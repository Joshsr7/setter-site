Setter Consulting Services LLC designs and runs automated scheduling, messaging, and phone-confirmation systems for small businesses across South Florida — so the work happens without anyone having to remember to do it.

What we build
01

Appointment confirmations

Automated calls and texts that confirm each day's appointments, capture the response, and flag the ones that need a human.

02

Scheduling & messaging

Two-way SMS and voice systems that handle booking, rescheduling, and reminders in the customer's own language.

03

Integrations

Connections to the calendar, CRM, and field-service tools a business already runs on, so the automation fits the existing workflow.

About

Setter Consulting Services LLC is a Florida-registered software and communication consultancy based in Boca Raton. We work with small, service-based businesses — repair companies, schools, studios, and local shops — that lose time and revenue to manual phone and scheduling work.

Our systems use standard telephony and messaging infrastructure to place and receive calls, send text reminders, and keep a business's calendar accurate, with a person always in the loop for anything that isn't routine.
