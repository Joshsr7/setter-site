SMS Privacy Policy

Setter Consulting Services · Last updated August 2026

This SMS Privacy Policy explains how text message communications are handled by Setter Consulting Services LLC ("Setter Consulting Services," "we," "us"). Setter Consulting Services sends SMS appointment confirmation messages on behalf of the service businesses it works with.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in data with any third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is used solely to communicate with you about your scheduled service appointments.

Information we collect

When you schedule a service appointment with a business Setter works with, we collect your name, phone number, and appointment details. Your phone number is used to send you text messages related to that appointment.

How we use text messaging

We send SMS messages to confirm, remind, or reschedule your scheduled service appointments. These are transactional service messages, not marketing messages. Examples include:

  • Confirming that you will be home for a scheduled technician visit
  • Reminding you of an upcoming appointment
  • Coordinating a reschedule if needed

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on the number of appointments you have scheduled. You will typically receive messages only around the time of a scheduled service visit.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply. These charges come from your mobile carrier and are not controlled by Setter Consulting Services LLC.

How you consent

You opt in through our web form at setterconsulting.com/sms-optin, where you enter your mobile number and check a box, unchecked by default, agreeing to receive SMS appointment confirmations. Consent to receive these messages is not a condition of purchase.

How to opt out

You may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive. After you reply STOP, you will receive one confirmation message and then no further texts. For help, reply HELP or contact us using the details below.

Data sharing

We do not share your mobile number or messaging consent with third parties except as required to deliver the messages (for example, our telecommunications provider) or as required by law. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
